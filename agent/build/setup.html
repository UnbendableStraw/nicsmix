ROOM LAYOUT

The dashed circle is the sound field, front up. Drag the room to move the speakers in it · scroll / pinch to grow or shrink · ⌥-drag, ⇧-scroll or two-finger twist to rotate · drag a speaker to fine-tune it (snaps to the centre and the wall, ⇧ to skip) · drag its tick to turn it · ◎ listener. Changes play live and save when you let go.

Soundcheck plays chirps from every speaker in turn (about a minute per speaker) and measures positions, orientation, walls and room EQ on the speakers themselves; playback is paused meanwhile. Keep the room quiet. ×3 runs it three times and solves the layout from the median of the measurements — use it when a single run looks off. Results are stored by this firmware — no Syng account involved.

FIRMWARE

…

The speakers keep two system slots (p2 / p3). An update is written to the idle one, read back and verified, your Troubadour files (agent, LED daemon, cloud block, keys, root password) are carried over, then the boot slot is switched with U-Boot's upgrade_available set, so a slot that does not come up is undone by the bootloader on the next boot. Settings, rooms, Wi-Fi and calibration live on the data partition and survive. Images come from tools/fw/build-troubadour.sh (troubadour-rootfs-<artifact>.img.gz, next to image.sdcard). Room-wide: Send copies the image to every member, Install on all updates the secondaries one by one and this primary last.

CONTROL RING & SOUNDS

Ring steps = how many ring positions span the volume range (stock 100 — fewer = coarser, more = finer). Direction restarts the ring daemon (hidserver) on this cell only; the UI sound level is what Ping and the setup chimes play at (stock 25 %).

HOUSEKEEPING

…

Everything here is applied through the speaker's own state service (cloudsyncd PatchState) — the same path Syng's cloud uses — so celldirector, the spatialiser and the SyFi link react as they would to the app. Nothing resets, nothing touches volume.
