ROOM LAYOUT

Drag a speaker to move it · drag its tick to turn it · dashed circle = the sound-field reference circle (drag its centre, edge or ◆ to rotate) · ◎ = listener. Front of the room is up.

Soundcheck plays chirps from every speaker in turn (about a minute per speaker) and measures positions, orientation, walls and room EQ on the speakers themselves. Keep the room quiet. Results are stored by this firmware — no Syng account involved.

Advanced

Everything here is applied through the speaker's own state service (cloudsyncd PatchState) — the same path Syng's cloud uses — so celldirector, the spatialiser and the SyFi link react as they would to the app. Nothing resets, nothing touches volume.
